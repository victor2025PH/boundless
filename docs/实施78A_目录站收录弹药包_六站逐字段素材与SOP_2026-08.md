## 六、FAQ（多数站有 FAQ / Q&A 区，直接粘）

**Is ChatX free?**
Yes — the download is a free tier, not a timed trial. Standard translation is unlimited and free,
plus 1,000 tokens per month and 10,000 bonus tokens on signup. More AI usage is pay-as-you-go.

**Which channels does it support?**
Telegram, WhatsApp, Messenger, LINE, Zalo and Instagram in one inbox.

**Do I need an API key or a GPU?**
Neither. AI inference runs through a managed gateway on cloud or LAN nodes, so there is no key
for you to manage — a regular office PC is enough.

**Where is my data stored?**
Chat history, platform sessions and customer profiles stay in your local user folder on the
machine running the client; they are not routed through vendor servers. AI inference is the
exception — by default it goes through our managed gateway, and it can be pointed at your own
endpoint or an on-prem model node instead. Either way the chat data itself never leaves your
machine, and the whole stack can be self-hosted.

**How is it different from respond.io or Intercom?**
Those are cloud SaaS help desks billed by subscription or per contact. ChatX is self-hostable,
priced by usage, and its AI is built to nurture relationships and close deals rather than resolve
tickets. See the side-by-side comparison at https://bd2026.cc/en/compare

**Is it compliant with AI disclosure rules?**
AI disclosure (nine languages), honest-identity mode and a crisis-referral loop ship as switches,
aligned with EU AI Act Art. 50 and California SB 243.

## 七、措辞红线（与实施77B 同一套，目录站尤其严）

1. **不写收益数字**（「earn $X/month」类），也不写未经审计的客户数量或增长率；
2. **竞品只写公开资料的一般性理解**，具体功能与价格一律指回对方官网；
3. **不勾 Cloud/SaaS 部署类型**（见 §四），那与我们的核心卖点自相矛盾；
4. 截图必须标注 demo data（见 §零）；
5. 不提 gated 词（换脸 / 智控 / 群控），目录站资料与主站合规口径一致。
6. **数据与 AI 推理必须分开讲**（2026-08-28 拍板，已同步官网）：聊天记录/会话/客户资料
   留本机；AI 推理默认走托管网关、可改指向自有端点或内网模型节点。
   **不要写「nothing routes through vendor servers」这类笼统句**——官网 FAQ 曾同页并存
   两种说法（一条说走我们的云网关、一条说什么都不经我们服务器），而这恰好砸在「数据主权」
   这个核心卖点上。目录站会把这段原样镜像到几十个站点，改回来极难。事实依据：托管网关＝
   `engines/chengjie/src/ai/hosted_gateway.py` + 官网 `/api/ai/*` 七个路由；自有端点＝
   引擎 config 的 `base_url`（如内网模型节点）。官网 `website/lib/chatxContent.ts` 的
   「我的账号和聊天数据存在哪里」已按同一口径改写，两边一致。
